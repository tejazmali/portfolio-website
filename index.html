About Me

My journey started with curiosity—curiosity about how websites work, how systems communicate, and how technology can solve real-world problems. Over time, this curiosity turned into passion. I actively work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I'm continuously expanding my skills while balancing my studies in robotics and engineering concepts.

I'm always eager to learn new tools, frameworks, and technologies, and I enjoy challenging myself with projects that push my limits. From building interactive websites to understanding robotic systems, I focus on growing consistently and developing problem-solving skills that matter in the real world.

My goal is to become a skilled developer and technologist who can create meaningful, user-focused solutions. I'm driven by innovation, continuous learning, and the excitement of turning ideas into reality—one project at a time.
